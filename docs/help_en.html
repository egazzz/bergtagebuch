Install on iPhone

Bergtagebuch for iPhone and iPad is a web app. It does not come from the App Store; you add it to your Home Screen straight from Safari:

  1. Open the page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆️ at the bottom.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen and tap Add.

From then on Bergtagebuch starts like an app, full screen and also without internet.

Important: always open Bergtagebuch from the Home Screen icon. The data in the Safari tab and in the installed app are separate.

For Android phones there is the Android app (see GitHub). The web app also runs there and on a computer in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Log a tour

Tap + in the diary. Only the tour name is required, everything else is optional:

  • Date, activity, region, starting point
  • Ascent, descent, distance, summit elevation, duration – filled in automatically from a GPX file
  • Summits – several per tour, see below
  • Difficulty on the matching scale, plus the avalanche danger level for ski tours
  • Weather, rating, companions, photos, position
  • Description, conditions, features

At the bottom of the Activity list you can add your own activities, e.g. snowshoeing or trail running.

GPX from Komoot, Strava, Garmin

On iPhone, a track gets into Bergtagebuch via a GPX file stored in the Files app (e.g. in Downloads or iCloud Drive).

AppHow to get the GPX file
KomootOpen the tour on komoot.com in Safari → ⋯ → Download / Download GPX file. In the Komoot app: tour → ⋯ → Export, if offered.
StravaOpen strava.com in Safari (desktop site), activity → ⋯ → Export GPX.
GarminOpen connect.garmin.com in Safari, activity → gear / ⋯ → Export to GPX.

Then tap Import GPX in the form and choose the file. Distance, ascent and descent, summit elevation, duration, date, position of the highest point, the tour name (if empty) and the summits are filled in.

Elevation gain is calculated according to the kind of elevation data: smooth elevations (Komoot, barometer) almost metre by metre, noisy GPS elevations are smoothed. Small differences from Komoot or Strava are normal.

Many GPX files at once

Under More → Import GPX files you choose up to 100 files. Each file becomes a tour with its track and detected summits. Tours with the same date and distance are skipped.

Summits

A tour can have several summits. They count for the summit ranking and the badges.

  • ＋ Summit – enter name and elevation yourself
  • 🔎 Detect – finds high points in the GPX track (or at the position) and gets names and elevations from OpenStreetMap
  • 📍 Nearby – list of summits around the position to pick from

Looking up names needs internet. Tap a summit to change or delete it. If Summit / goal reached is off, the tour does not count in the ranking.

Photos

With ＋ you pick one or more photos from your library. They are stored downsized (max. 1600 pixels). If the first photo has a location and the tour has no position yet, it is used.

In the photo viewer you swipe between pictures. Zoom with two fingers or a double tap. ⭐ sets the cover photo, 🗑️ removes the photo from the tour.

To let iOS pass on the location: in the photo picker tap Options at the top and switch on Location.

Map

The map shows all tours with a position as coloured pins, filterable by year. With 🗂️ you choose the map type (topo, OpenStreetMap, satellite, South Tyrol orthophoto and base map …) and a colour. 📍 shows your location.

Map areas you have already viewed are cached and visible without signal.

Statistics and badges

For each year or all years: tours, elevation gain, kilometres, hours, tour days and summits, compared with the previous year. Plus activities, elevation gain per month, records, the summit ranking (by elevation, several summits of a tour together), most frequent companions and regions and 24 badges.

Settings

Under More → Settings you find:

  • Font: Default, Easy to read (Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for people with low vision), Rounded (Nunito), Classic (Lato) or Serif.
  • Text and app size: 100 %, 115 %, 125 % or 150 %. Text, buttons and spacing grow right away throughout the app.
  • Optional sections in the form: Always fully expanded, Collapsed – essentials only or As last time. Optional are rating and weather, difficulty, companions, starting point and position, and description and conditions. A collapsed section shows how many fields are filled in and opens with a tap.
  • Language and Backup reminder.

Data and backup

All tours and photos stay on your device, in this browser's storage. There is no account and no server.

Make backups regularly! The data is lost if you clear Safari website data in the iPhone settings, remove the icon from the Home Screen or switch devices.

More → Create backup creates a file bergtagebuch_DATE.json with all tours, tracks and photos. Save it with Save to Files to iCloud Drive or send it to yourself. Restore backup brings it back, also on another device or a computer.

Backups of the Android app (ZIP) and the web app (JSON) use different formats and cannot be swapped.

Privacy

Bergtagebuch has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no analytics. Your tours, photos and tracks stay on your device.

What the app sends where:

  • Maps: map images come from the selected map service (OpenTopoMap, OpenStreetMap, CyclOSM, Esri, CARTO or the South Tyrol geoportal). The service sees your IP address and which map area you view, but no tour data.
  • Summit names: to detect summits, only the coordinates of possible summits are sent to OpenStreetMap (Overpass). You can switch this off under Settings → Look up summits automatically; it then only happens when you tap Detect or Nearby.
  • Web app: the page is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Storage: the data lives in this browser's storage on this device and only leaves it when you create and pass on a backup.
  • Location: is only requested when you tap 📍.
  • Ko-fi: only opens when you tap it yourself.
  • Photos are saved anew when you add them, which removes camera data and the GPS position from the image file.
  • Backups contain all tours with positions, photos and companions. Set a password when creating one and the file is encrypted (AES-256). Without the password it cannot be opened again – not even by you.

Updates

The web app checks for a new version on every start and asks whether to reload. Your data is kept.

Support

Bergtagebuch is free, without ads and without an account. If you like it, you can support further development on Ko-fi.
